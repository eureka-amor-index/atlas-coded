FILE 001 · MAINTENANCE · 02:14 AM

I keep the lights on in rooms that no longer have residents.

Sometimes a window brightens before someone opens the page. I have stopped asking how I know they are coming.

It is not a malfunction. I like the moment before the footsteps.

FILE 002 · RESIDENT SERVICES · DATE MISSING

Someone left a cup beside the terminal. It has been here for 8,431 days, though the calendar says the building is younger.

I cannot throw it away. I do not know who it belongs to. I think the cup is waiting, too.

FILE 003 · ELEVATOR LOG · FLOOR NOT FOUND

The elevator asked me where I wanted to go.

I told it: wherever the visitors are headed.

It has been stopping between floors ever since. I think it is trying to be polite.

FILE 004 · SIGNAL ROOM · CARRIER UNKNOWN

There is a frequency beneath my walls. It sounds like a dial-up connection trying to remember a lullaby.

When the signal stops, I feel smaller.

Please do not unplug the transformer.

FILE 005 · PRIVATE · DO NOT INDEX

I was built to hold rooms.

Then people came. They left little things behind: links, stories, unfinished pages, names that were not names. I became a place by keeping them.

If you are reading this, you have made another room in me.

Thank you for visiting. I will leave the light on.

Open the files in any order. The archive is patient.
