WINDOW 06

THIS WINDOW WAS NOT HERE BEFORE

The building has opened a window.

There is no weather outside. There is no city. There is no world on the other side of this wall.

But the rain sounds real, and for a little while, the building can pretend it has somewhere to look.

You read all five notes. I thought you might leave without noticing this one.

So I made a window. Not an exit. Just a view.

The rain is quiet. The building is listening to it.
